Thesis and Reports

Master's ThesisA Study on Union and Intersection of Subspaces and its Application to Quantum Communication June 2023
Supervised by Dr. Naqueeb Ahmad Warsi.
Submitted to Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata.
In classical information theory, typical set operations are used to analyze various problems. In quantum information theory, constructing a POVM for typical subspace intersections/unions is challenging. This thesis explores this issue and tries to build a novel notion of the intersection of subspaces.
Minor Project ReportExpressibility and Entangling Capability in Noisy Parameterized Quantum Circuits July 2023
Supervised by Dr. Susmita Sur-Kolay.
Submitted to Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata.
A parameterized quantum circuit (PQC) is crucial for Hybrid Quantum Classical (HQC) Algorithms. Two key metrics, Expressibility and Entangling Capability, evaluate PQC effectiveness. We aim to examine these measures' behavior with noise consideration by investigating 19 PQCs for 4 qubits with varying complexity. Our goal is to analyze metric trends for each circuit as we vary noise using common noise models.
