Ayanava Dasgupta is a Senior Research Fellow and doctoral candidate in Computer Science at the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata, supervised by Dr. Naqueeb Ahmad Warsi. He holds an M.Tech in Computer Science from ISI Kolkata and a B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering from Government College of Engineering and Leather Technology (GCELT), Kolkata. A recipient of the prestigious TCS Research Fellowship (Cycle 2025-29), his research investigates quantum communication, quantum learning theory, quantum statistical inference and quantum differential privacy etc.

Research Focus

Primary Fields of Work

01

Quantum Statistical Inference

Pure-state prediction under relative-entropy loss, Cramér-Rao efficiency of quantum U-statistics, and optimal quantum state estimation.

02

Quantum Differential Privacy

Quantum information ordering, stability-implies-generalization guarantees, and privacy-preserving quantum learning.

03

Quantum Learning Theory

Generalization bounds for quantum learning via Rényi divergences, PAC learning in operator spaces, and sample complexity analysis.
